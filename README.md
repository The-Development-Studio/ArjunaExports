# Terra Narrative

ARJUNA EXPORTS — IMMERSIVE STORYTELLING WEBSITE REDESIGN

Design a complete, premium, highly visual, storytelling-driven B2B export website for Arjuna Exports, a manufacturer and exporter of coconut-based growing media and related products.

Reference the existing business content and information architecture from:

arjunaexports.com

The redesign must NOT look like a conventional agriculture website, generic corporate template, SaaS website, or card-heavy product catalogue.

The new experience should feel like an interactive visual documentary about the journey of coconut-based growing media — from nature in South India, through processing and manufacturing, to professional growers around the world.

01 — CORE CREATIVE CONCEPT

Concept

“From Nature. To Growth. Across the World.”

Build the entire website around one continuous narrative:

Coconut Plantation → Coconut Husk → Fibre & Pith → Processing → Coco Growing Media → Agriculture → Healthy Plants → Global Export → Sustainable Growth

Every major section should feel like another chapter of the same story.

The user should understand Arjuna Exports primarily through:

Photography

Cinematic compositions

Product close-ups

Macro textures

Scroll interactions

Manufacturing imagery

Agricultural environments

Typography

Motion

Data visualization

Minimal supporting copy

Target visual-content balance:

65–75% visual storytelling
15–25% typography/content
10% interface/data/graphics

Do not solve every section with cards.

Create a website that positions Arjuna Exports not simply as another coco peat exporter, but as a sophisticated international manufacturer transforming a natural resource into professional growing solutions.

The final experience should feel like a combination of:

Nature documentary

Premium editorial publication

Modern manufacturing story

International B2B product experience
